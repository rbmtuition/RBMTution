## Photo guidelines

- Avoid showing children’s faces without parent or guardian consent. Over-the-shoulder shots, hands, notebooks and study materials work well.
- Do not include watermarks, other coaching brand names or visible personal documents.
- Use natural light where possible. Avoid heavy filters and phone beautification modes.
- Keep the style consistent: warm, natural and authentic, matching the site’s navy and gold palette.
- For online tuition, make sure no student names, email addresses, phone numbers or other personal data are visible on the laptop screen.
- For office and team photos, remove clutter and ensure any visible signage is clean and current.
